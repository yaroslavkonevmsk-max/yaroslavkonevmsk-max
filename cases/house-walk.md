# House Walk

**Explore the exterior. Step inside. Change the scene.**

The profile cover is an AI-assisted concept visualization based on this house. The image below is a capture from the working 3D demo.

![Current House Walk browser demo in daylight, with orbit and walk controls](../assets/house-walk-day.jpg)

### The brief

Make a house easier to explore on the web. Visitors should be able to understand the exterior, enter the property, and compare the atmosphere of different settings within one interface.

### The work

A real-time WebGL property demo brings together:

- Exterior orbit and walking modes.
- Exploration of the grounds and first floor.
- An interactive entrance door.
- Day and evening settings.
- Furnishing variants.

The interface places the entry point, navigation modes, and scene controls around the model. The image above is an actual browser capture of the current unbranded demo, not a separate architectural render.

### The outcome

A working local demonstration of an interactive property presentation. Visitors can move from an overview into a first-floor walk and explore scene options instead of relying on a fixed set of views.

### Scope

The exterior depicts a two-storey house; the walkthrough covers the grounds and first floor only. The interiors and surroundings are demonstrative. This case documents the current demo stage and does not include a public live build or a release of the underlying models and assets.
