# House Walk

**Could you see your life unfolding here?**

A floor plan tells you where the rooms are. House Walk gives you a way to explore how the place might feel. Circle the house, approach the entrance and move through the first floor at your own pace. Return in evening light, then try a different furnishing option. The story changes because you are the one moving through it.

Explore the working experience below, with navigation and scene controls close at hand.

![Current House Walk browser demo in daylight, with orbit and walk controls](../assets/house-walk-day.jpg)

### The experience

The experience lets visitors orbit the exterior, explore the grounds, open the entrance door and walk the first floor. Day and evening settings and furnishing variants help them picture more than one way to inhabit the space. The interface keeps navigation and scene controls close without crowding the model.

The image above shows the working browser interface.

### What this case shows

The walkthrough covers the grounds and first floor of a two-storey house. This case presents a local demonstration; a public interactive build and the underlying assets are not included.
