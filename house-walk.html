House Walk

Interactive property experience

Could you picture
your life here?

Spatial navigation · Interactive controls · Property presentation

The experience

A home to explore.
A life to imagine.

How would it feel to arrive here? To cross the garden, open the door and choose where to linger? House Walk lets a visitor find their own route through a home.

Circle the exterior, explore the grounds and wander through the first floor. Switch from daylight to evening and try furnishing options. Each choice offers another way to imagine living in the space.

The working experience covers the grounds and first floor. The view below shows its browser interface, with navigation and scene controls close at hand.
