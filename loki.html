Loki

Cinematic architectural website

A house revealed like a short film.

Interface composition · Responsive layout · Scroll motion

The experience

Let the setting
tell the story.

First, the forest. Then the glow of a house between the trees. Keep scrolling and the story moves indoors, from the exterior into warm rooms connected to the landscape around them.

Loki gives architecture the pacing of a film. The visitor sets the rhythm, with each new view given room to make an impression. Four chapters carry the journey from setting to interior.

The experience combines a rendered image sequence, scroll motion and responsive layouts. The selected frames below are from the working project.
